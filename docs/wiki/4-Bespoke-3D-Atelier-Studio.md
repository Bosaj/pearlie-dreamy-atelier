# 4. Bespoke 3D Atelier Studio (`BespokeStudio.tsx`)

The **Bespoke 3D Atelier Configurator** (`src/components/BespokeStudio.tsx`) enables clients to design a custom Pearlie creation and send a structured specification directly to Pearlie's WhatsApp Concierge (`https://wa.me/message/HYSIBOQVMTCAE1`).

## 6-Step Customization Pipeline

1. **Step 01 · Choose Silhouette (`SILHOUETTES`)**:
   - *Crystal Kiss-Lock Couture Clutch* (`450 MAD`, `1,200 beads`, `~18 hrs`)
   - *Celestine Diamond-Lattice Tote* (`390 MAD`, `880 beads`, `~15 hrs`)
   - *Custom 3D Alphabet Keychain (A–Z)* (`95 MAD`, `185 beads`, `~3 hrs`)
   - *Coquette Pearl & Crystal Bow Charm* (`90 MAD`, `160 beads`, `~2.5 hrs`)
   - *Iridescent Pearl AirPods Couture Case* (`190 MAD`, `420 beads`, `~7 hrs`)
2. **Step 02 · Select 3D Pearl Initial (`A–Z`)**:
   - Interactive 26-letter grid allowing clients to pick their custom woven initial charm.
3. **Step 03 · Pearl & Crystal Color Palette (`PALETTES`)**:
   - *Ivory Nacre & Lavender*
   - *Noir Seraphim Onyx*
   - *Ruby Empress Crimson*
   - *Celestine Seafoam Mint*
   - *Amber Honey Topaz*
   - *Champagne Lumina Gold*
   - *Blush Coquette Rose*
4. **Step 04 · Hardware & Satin Ribbon Finish (`HARDWARE_OPTIONS`)**:
   - *24k Gold Kiss-Lock + Detachable Gold Chain*
   - *Elarielle Lavender Satin Bow + Gold Clasp*
   - *Silver Rhodium Clasp + Pearl Wristlet*
   - *Ivory Silk Ribbon + Pearl Handle*
5. **Step 05 · Moroccan Delivery City (`MOROCCAN_CITIES`)**:
   - Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Oujda, Berkane, Tangier, Fes, Meknes, Agadir, Tetouan, Kenitra, El Jadida, Nador, Settat, Mohammedia, and All Morocco (`🚚🇲🇦`).
6. **Step 06 · Signature Gift Card Message**:
   - Custom note printed on the signature Pearlie thank-you card inside the lavender-ribbon gift box.
