## 🌟 Key Experiences & Pillars

1. **Interactive 3D Pearl & Crystal Light-Refraction Field (`PearlFieldCanvas.tsx`):**
   - Real-time 60fps HTML5 Canvas simulation rendering floating mother-of-pearl and faceted crystal spheres with cursor spring-repulsion physics and constellation filaments.
   - 5 switchable light spectrums: *Nacre Lavender* (`nacre`), *Ruby Empress* (`ruby`), *Noir Seraphim* (`noir`), *Celestine Mint* (`mint`), and *Amber Honey* (`amber`).

2. **La Boutique Pearlie (`#boutique`):**
   - Complete curated archive of **16 handcrafted creations** (`PRL-001` through `PRL-016`) across four signature lines:
     - **Crystal Kiss-Lock Clutches** (*Noir Seraphim*, *Ruby Empress*, *Midnight Onyx Beaded-Arch*, *Wild Leopard*, *Amber Honey*, *Ivory Lumina Champagne*).
     - **Celestine & Elarielle Pearl Totes** (*Celestine Siren Mint & Nacre*, *Celestine Diamond-Lattice*, *Elarielle Lavender Ribbon*, *Cloud Nine Mini Pearl Tote*).
     - **Alphabet A–Z & Coquette Keychains** (*Custom 3D Pearl Initial A–Z*, *Micro Pearl Tote Keychain*, *Coquette Pearl & Crystal Bow*, *Puffed Ruby Crystal Heart*, *Daisy Pearl Charm*).
     - **Mini Cases & Pouches** (*Iridescent Pearl AirPods Case*).
   - Multi-photo inspection modal, bead counts, artisan weaving hours, MAD pricing, and instant search/filter.

3. **Bespoke 3D Atelier Configurator (`BespokeStudio.tsx`):**
   - 6-step interactive customizer allowing clients across Morocco (`🚚🇲🇦`) to select their silhouette, 3D pearl letter (`A–Z`), 7 pearl/crystal color palettes, hardware/ribbon finish, Moroccan delivery city, and signature thank-you card message.
   - Generates a structured atelier specification sheet for one-click submission to Pearlie's official WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/message/HYSIBOQVMTCAE1`).

4. **24-Post Instagram Lookbook (`#lookbook`):**
   - Every authentic post and reel from `@_pearlie.dreamy` (`DfX7Lo-sICS` through `Db8fJ2pMUFX`) bundled locally in `public/images/` for zero-latency browsing.

5. **Little Calm Corner ☁️ Sanctuary (`CalmCornerPortal.tsx`):**
   - Dedicated portal for Ihsane's sister sanctuary [`@__little.calmcorner`](https://www.instagram.com/__little.calmcorner/) (*“🤍 little reminders for your soul | 🌸 soft life & calm mind”*).
   - Includes the **Daily Soft Reminder Deck**, an interactive **4–4–6 Calm Breathing Orb**, her **3 Behind-the-Scenes Story Highlights** (*My business 💎*, *Soft Planner 🤍*, *Cozy Breaks 🎬*), and **5 Reels & Posts** including her ENIAD engineering school integration day.

6. **Diwan al-Durr — Classical Arabic Poetry & Moroccan Maghribi Calligraphy (`MaghribiPoetryDiwan.tsx`):**
   - Dedicated Arabic poetic pavilion (`#diwan`) featuring **4 bespoke classical Arabic poems** (*Bahr al-Kamil*, *Bahr al-Basit*, *Bahr al-Wafir*, and *Al-Muwashshah al-Maghribi*) composed in honor of **Ihsane (`@ihsane__ft`)**, **Pearlie Handcrafted**, and **Little Calm Corner**.
   - Bundled with the authentic Moroccan **`Bouazzi Maghribi`** script (`public/fonts/BouazziMaghribi-Regular.woff2`) plus **`Aref Ruqaa`** and **`Amiri`**, complete with a live 3-script switcher (`الخط المغربي` / `خط الرقعة` / `الخط الأميري`), translation toggle, and 1-click poem copy.

7. **Founder Surprise Dedication (`FounderSurpriseModal.tsx`) & Universal Phone Responsiveness (`320px–430px`):**
   - Accessible from the top navbar (**“👑 For Ihsane ✨”**) and founder section, presenting a trilingual (`EN` / `FR` / `ع`) surprise dedication letter, Maghribi calligraphic couplets, and celebratory pearl sparkles.
   - Engineered for 100% mobile phone responsiveness (`320px` through `430px` viewports) with zero horizontal overflow in both `LTR` and `RTL`.
